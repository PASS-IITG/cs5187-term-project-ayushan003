\section*{Author Contributions}
\label{sec:contributions}

\paragraph{Anonymity and signing.} FSE reviews papers double-anonymously, so an
FSE submission would leave out the names below. The course instead requires each
member to justify their contribution with a signed statement in the report.
This report therefore names its authors, and this statement is signed by both.
That is its only departure from the FSE format.

\paragraph{Statement.} This project was carried out by Ayush Singh Chauhan
(240101117) and Mehul Raj (240101125) in shared working sessions. Both members
took part in the design, the implementation, the campaigns, the triage of every
candidate and the preparation of both patches, and both can explain every part
of the work. Both upstream pull requests were submitted from one member's
account. Each area below was led by the named member, who drove its design and
wrote its first version, and was reviewed and extended by the other.

\paragraph{Ayush Singh Chauhan.} The corpus pipeline and the templating step
(Section~\ref{sec:step-mine}); the engine adapter and its error classification
(Section~\ref{sec:impl}); the campaign driver; and the upstream submissions.

\paragraph{Mehul Raj.} Capability probing and the typed generator
(Section~\ref{sec:step-run}); the crash oracle and its planted-defect check; the
triage pipeline (Section~\ref{sec:step-triage}); and the evaluation.

\paragraph{Joint.} The choice of target and corpus source, the root-cause
analysis of all four defects, both patches, the test suite and this paper.
